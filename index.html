Build your own THB emissions scenario

This site allows you to build your own climate scenario and compare it to the newly-released CMIP7 scenarios that will underlie climate research and policy for many years. The tool is based on methods I first described in my book The Climate Fix (2010) and have expanded on in recent years at The Honest Broker at Substack. Explore the tool and then tell me how to improve it (or if you find any errors). Honest brokering is a group activity.
